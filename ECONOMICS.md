# Unit Economics & Business Model: SpendLens

SpendLens is designed as a high-margin, low-overhead lead generation and affiliate engine. By offering immense upfront value (a deterministic financial audit), we earn trust and the right to recommend partner products.

## The Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Because we utilize serverless infrastructure and highly optimized API calls, our cost per user is microscopic.

| Item | Cost | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Vercel Hosting** | $0.00 | Free tier covers 100GB bandwidth, sufficient for ~50,000 users/mo. |
| **Supabase DB** | $0.00 | Free tier covers 500MB database space (millions of audit records). |
| **Resend Emails** | $0.00 | Free tier covers 3,000 emails/month. |
| **Gemini 2.5 Flash API** | ~$0.0001 | A typical audit sends ~500 input tokens and receives ~150 output tokens. Flash pricing is $0.075/1M input and $0.30/1M output. Cost per audit is negligible. |
| **Total Cost Per Audit** | **<$0.01** | It costs us practically nothing to service a user. |

## Revenue Generation

We do not charge a subscription fee. We monetize through high-value B2B affiliate commissions and lead generation.

### 1. Affiliate Partnerships (e.g., Credex)
Our primary monetization engine is recommending consolidated infrastructure.
- **Scenario:** A startup is paying $100/mo directly to Anthropic and $100/mo to OpenAI.
- **Recommendation:** We recommend they route their API calls through **Credex** to save 30% via wholesale pricing.
- **Revenue:** SpendLens earns a 20% recurring affiliate commission on the revenue generated by that startup via Credex. 

**Expected Value:** If a startup spends $500/mo on APIs through Credex, we earn $100/mo *in recurring revenue* per converted user.

### 2. Lead Selling / Sponsorships
As we build a database of thousands of founders actively optimizing their SaaS stacks, our email list becomes incredibly valuable.
- We can sell sponsored placements in our weekly "SaaS Optimization" newsletter to B2B software companies.
- ECPM (Effective Cost Per Mille) for highly targeted B2B founder lists can reach $50 - $100.

## The Economics Equation
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** $0.00 (Driven entirely by organic SEO, Product Hunt, and the viral sharing loop).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Highly variable, but assuming a 2% conversion rate to a $50/mo affiliate payout, the blended LTV per visitor is ~$1.00.
- **Margin:** 99.9% 

By keeping our COGS at zero and relying on organic growth loops, SpendLens is a highly profitable, scalable micro-SaaS.
