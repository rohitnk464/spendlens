# Go-To-Market Strategy: SpendLens

SpendLens is a B2B micro-SaaS aimed at founders, indie hackers, and small agency owners who are burning cash on redundant AI tools. Our goal is to acquire high-intent users efficiently without a massive marketing budget.

## 1. Initial Launch Strategy (Days 1-30)

### Product Hunt Launch
- Position: "Stop overpaying for AI. Audit your SaaS stack in 60 seconds."
- Goal: Top 3 Product of the Day.
- Offer: Free personalized AI audit report.

### Hacker News & Reddit (`r/SaaS`, `r/Entrepreneur`)
- Strategy: Value-driven text posts rather than direct links. 
- Example Title: *"I analyzed 500 startup SaaS stacks. Here's how much money they waste on overlapping AI tools."*
- Call to action: "I built a free tool to check your own stack: [SpendLens]"

### X (Twitter) Build-in-Public
- Share the exact prompts and logic we use to classify AI tools.
- Share anonymized aggregated data: "Did you know 40% of people paying for Claude Pro also pay for ChatGPT Plus? You probably don't need both."

## 2. Lead Generation & Viral Loop

### The "Share Your Savings" Engine
The core viral loop of SpendLens is built directly into the Audit Results page. 
When a user sees they can save $1,200/year, they are prompted to share their results.
- **Dynamic OG Tags**: When they paste their link in Twitter or Slack, the preview card dynamically generates a title: *"I saved $1,200 on AI tools using SpendLens!"*
- This creates curiosity and drives their network to try the tool.

### Newsletter Lead Capture
At the bottom of the audit report, we capture the user's email to send them their report. This builds our proprietary list of founders who we know are actively looking to optimize SaaS spend.

## 3. SEO & Content Strategy (Months 1-3)

We will programmatically generate SEO-optimized comparison pages based on our `pricing-data.ts`.
- `ChatGPT vs Claude: Which is cheaper for coding?`
- `Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Cost analysis`
- `Best AI Coding Assistants 2026`

These pages will capture high-intent Google search traffic and funnel them directly into the SpendLens audit form.

## 4. Monetization & Partnerships

We don't charge the user for the audit. Instead, we monetize via **B2B affiliate partnerships**.
- If we recommend a user switches from ChatGPT ($20/mo) to an aggregated platform like **Credex**, we provide an affiliate link.
- **Credex Partnership:** We heavily promote Credex credits as a way to get wholesale API pricing, taking a percentage of the transaction or a flat referral fee.
