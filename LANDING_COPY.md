# Landing Page Copy Framework: SpendLens

Our landing page is designed to convert visitors into users by immediately identifying their pain point (wasting money on AI subscriptions) and offering a frictionless, instant solution.

## 1. The Hero Section (The "Hook")
**Framework:** PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)
- **Header:** "Stop Overpaying for AI Tools."
- **Sub-header:** "You are probably paying for 3 different AI tools that do the exact same thing. Enter your stack in 60 seconds and let our audit engine find you cheaper, better alternatives."
- **Primary CTA:** "Start Free Audit" (High contrast, pulsing animation to draw the eye).
- **Secondary Proof:** "Trusted by 500+ founders."

## 2. The "How It Works" Section (The "Logic")
We break down the perceived complexity of auditing software into 3 dead-simple steps so the user doesn't feel overwhelmed.
1. **Input Your Stack:** "Tell us what you use. (ChatGPT, Midjourney, GitHub Copilot...)"
2. **We Run the Math:** "Our deterministic engine analyzes overlaps and calculates exact wholesale pricing."
3. **Keep the Change:** "Cancel the bloat and get your personalized savings report."

## 3. Social Proof & Authority (The "Trust")
- We display a "Live Savings Ticker" or testimonials.
- "Founders save an average of $1,240/year."
- Mentioning the specific technologies we audit (Anthropic, OpenAI, Midjourney) borrows their brand authority.

## 4. The FAQ (Objection Handling)
We systematically destroy the reasons why a user *wouldn't* use the tool.
- **Objection:** Is this going to take forever?
  - **Answer:** "No, it takes less than 60 seconds."
- **Objection:** Do I have to connect my credit card or bank account?
  - **Answer:** "Absolutely not. We just ask you what you use."
- **Objection:** How does SpendLens make money?
  - **Answer:** "We are entirely free for you. We make money by referring you to discounted infrastructure partners."

## 5. The Footer CTA (The "Last Chance")
- **Header:** "Ready to stop burning runway?"
- **CTA:** "Audit My Stack Now"
