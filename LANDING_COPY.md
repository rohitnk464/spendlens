## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How does SpendLens calculate my savings?
We use a fully deterministic pricing database that is constantly updated with official rates from all major AI providers (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Anthropic, Gemini, Windsurf). The engine evaluates your team size, use case, and current plans across four distinct checks (plan sizing, cheaper alternatives, API arbitrage, and Credex credit discounts) to find exact, mathematically sound overlaps.

### Q2: Do I have to connect my bank account or credit card?
No! SpendLens is fully anonymous and privacy-first. We do not integrate with Plaid, require bank statements, or demand credit cards. You simply select the tools you currently use and your team size in our 30-second wizard, and we calculate your savings instantly.

### Q3: Is my data safe?
Absolutely. All audits are completely anonymous and saved under random, highly secure UUIDs in our database. We enforce strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies in Supabase, meaning no client browser can read or write to other audits. Your email is only captured at the end of the flow if you willingly opt-in to receive your PDF report.

### Q4: What is the "Credex" discount recommended in the report?
Credex is our partner platform that sells discounted AI infrastructure credits for Cursor, Claude Pro, ChatGPT Enterprise, and raw developer APIs. By purchasing credit pools through Credex, startups and agencies can instantly save an additional 15% to 30% on their remaining subscriptions, which we calculate and factor directly into your audit results.

### Q5: How accurate are the alternative tool recommendations?
Very. Our alternative engine doesn't just recommend the cheapest tool; it cross-references your primary use case. For example, if your primary workflow is coding, we compare Cursor Pro vs. Copilot Pro vs. Windsurf. If your workflow is writing or general research, we recommend Gemini or Claude Pro, ensuring you only switch to alternatives that actually match your dev requirements.
